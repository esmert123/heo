Poster Presentation

No separate submission required

Posters are uploaded during the conference registration/submission process.

The Poster Competition is open to registered undergraduate and graduate students. Posters will be collected via the conference registration/submission form (no separate submission). Participating students will demonstrate their engagement in high-quality research with an ability to present their work professionally and effectively to the jury during the dedicated poster session.

Participation Requirements

  • In-person poster session (jury visits during the poster exhibition)
  • Eligible: registered undergraduate/graduate students (valid Student ID required)
  • Poster size: A0 (template available for download above)
  • Participants print & bring their own poster
  • Limit: 1 poster per participant

Evaluation Criterion

  • Background provides appropriate perspective/context
  • Objectives/research questions are clearly stated
  • Research design/methods/modeling is appropriate and transparent
  • Data sources and/or sampling procedures are clear
  • Research objectives are met/addressed
  • Factual information is kept separate from interpretations or implications
  • Abstract is presented in an unbiased manner
  • Clarity of presentation

Awards

1st 2nd 3rd

Participants who place 1st, 2nd, and 3rd will be awarded by our sponsors.

Selection of Jury Members

  • Prior to the conference, TriboSurf'26 holds a Call for Judges, where registered attendees can sign up to be an on-site research award judge.
  • Judges select their field of interest based on their research expertise.
  • Undergraduate and graduate students are not eligible to be jury members.

Art of the Micrograph

Showcase your most beautiful, innovative, or compelling micrographs related to tribology and surface characterization. The competition is open to all registered participants. Original micrographs only (not published earlier, not AI-generated).

Eligibility: Registered participants Entries: Up to 2 images Caption: ≤ 250 characters Quality: ≥ 300 dpi Formats: JPG / PNG / TIFF

How to Submit

Add caption + technique details

Categories

  • Most Aesthetic Microstructure
  • Most Innovative Imaging Technique
  • Best Storytelling (Image + Description)

Submission Details

Submission Details

  • Original micrographs only (not published earlier, not AI-generated)
  • Related to tribology / surface characterization
  • Images obtained via SEM, AFM, TEM, optical microscopy, etc.
  • Up to 2 images per participant
  • Provide images at ≥ 300 dpi in JPEG, PNG, or TIFF format
  • Provide a technical caption (≤ 250 characters)
  • Suggested caption content: technique, material/sample, magnification/scale bar, key feature, why it matters
  • Caption can also be provided in a PDF/Word document (optional)
  • Awarded images may be used on the cover page of the TriboSurf'26 Conference proceedings
