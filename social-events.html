Social Tours Included in Registration Fee

Sumela-Zigana Mountain-Hamsikoy Tour

November 7, 2026
As part of the social program, a Conference Tour will be organized with a full-day excursion to the most iconic natural and historical sites of the Eastern Black Sea region. The tour will depart from Osman Turan Kongre Merkezi at 09:30. After an approximately one-hour drive, participants will reach the closest accessible point to Sumela Monastery, from where local shuttle minibuses will be used to access the site, followed by a short walk to the monastery. Following the visit to Sumela Monastery, the tour will continue along the Zigana Mountain route, located at an altitude of approximately 2032 m, offering panoramic views and fresh mountain air. A short stop will be made for sightseeing and photography. The tour will then proceed to Hamsikoy, where participants will enjoy a break and taste the region's famous Hamsikoy Sutlac (rice pudding). At the end of the program, the tour will return to Trabzon.
