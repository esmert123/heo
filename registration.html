Accommodation Options

Trabzon offers many accommodation options; our official partner hotel with discounted conference rates is Koru Hotel.
Koru Hotel is a four-star property operating under the brand of Karadeniz Technical University and located within the main KTU campus. Designed with spacious and modern aesthetics, the hotel comprises a total of 110 rooms, including 10 suites, 5 family suites, 24 double (French) rooms, 59 standard twin rooms, 11 triple rooms, and 1 accessible room, catering to a wide range of accommodation needs. Special discounted room prices have been arranged exclusively for TRiboSurf'26 participants. To benefit from these conference-specific prices, participants are required to contact the hotel directly via e-mail or phone.
