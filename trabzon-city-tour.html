Trabzon City Tour

November 6, 2026
The Trabzon City Tour offers a well-designed itinerary covering the city's most important historical, cultural, and natural landmarks. The tour departs from Koru Hotel. The first stop is Ayasofya Mosque, a remarkable monument dating back to the Byzantine period and renowned for its architecture. The tour then continues to the Trabzon Aquarium, where participants can explore the rich marine life of the Black Sea region. A short break will be given at Boztepe, a popular panoramic viewpoint overlooking the city and the Black Sea. The program also includes a visit to Atatürk Mansion, where Mustafa Kemal Atatürk stayed during his visits to Trabzon. The tour concludes with a visit to Kızlar Monastery, one of the city's most significant historical and religious landmarks.
